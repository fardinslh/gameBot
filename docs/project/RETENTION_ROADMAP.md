# Follow the retention-first roadmap

Bale integration is intentionally delayed until Crown & Coin has enough visible progression and repeat-play content. Each item below is a separate bounded task; completing one never authorizes starting the next.

1. **Retention 01A — Building Evolution, Core**: Castle, Farm, Lumber Mill, Mine, and Grand Market. Implemented.
2. **Retention 01B — Advanced Building Evolution and Goals**: Academy, Blacksmith, Watchtower, Workshop, and Kingdom progression goals. Not implemented.
3. **Retention 02 — Missions, Achievements, Daily Return**. Not implemented.
4. **Retention 03 — Hero Expansion**. Not implemented.
5. **Retention 04 — PvE Campaign / Adventure**. Not implemented.
6. **Retention 05 — Shop MVP and Gem Economy**. Not implemented.
7. **Retention 06 — Guild MVP**. Not implemented.
8. **Retention 07 — Guild Cooperation**. Not implemented.
9. **Retention 08 — Player and Guild Leaderboards / Competitive Meta**. Not implemented.
10. **Retention 09 — Content, Balance, and Retention QA**. Not implemented.
11. **Bale staging integration**. Not implemented.
12. **Production readiness**. Not complete.
13. **30–50 player closed test**, real-user fixes, then soft launch.

The current quality gate still includes owner review of Aren and real-device audio. Retention work does not turn technical audio validation into subjective approval.
